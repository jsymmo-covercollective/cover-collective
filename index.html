ISBN lookup
Search by title or enter an ISBN and the details fill in automatically. You can also scan barcodes directly with your phone camera.
Status tracking
Kept, Loaned out, Given away, To read, or Read & returned — every book has a story, Cover Collective tracks it.
Lend books to friends
Loan a book to another Cover Collective user. It appears on their shelf as borrowed and returns to yours when they're done.
Timeline & yearly stats
A chronological view of your reading life, with yearly breakdowns, bar charts and your top-rated picks.
Series, genres & ratings
Tag books with genres, track series order, leave notes, and give star ratings. Filter and search your whole collection.
Works everywhere, offline too
Install Cover Collective as an app on any device. Your shelf syncs across all screens and works even without a connection.
